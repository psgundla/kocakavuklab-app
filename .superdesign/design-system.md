# Kocakavuk Lab Moments Design System

## Product context

Kocakavuk Lab is a computational oncology research group. The Moments page
documents lab life, scientific meetings, retreats, celebrations, and team
milestones. It must feel credible, warm, and easy for future maintainers to
update.

## Existing brand constraints

- Preserve the current black navigation and footer.
- Reuse the real white Kocakavuk Lab logo.
- Preserve the current gray-50 page background.
- Use gray-800/900 for headings and gray-500/600 for secondary copy.
- Use cyan only as a restrained accent.
- Use the existing sans-serif/Poppins typography.
- Do not introduce gradients, decorative serif fonts, or unrelated colors.

## Moments page direction

- Inspired by the chronological “Lab Life” structure at Verhaak Lab, without
  copying its branding.
- Large centered “Lab Life” or “Moments” heading with a concise subtitle.
- Vertical year-based timeline on desktop; single-column chronology on mobile.
- Each event contains date, title, optional short caption, and a responsive
  collage of real photos.
- Alternate event content around a central line only when it improves scanning;
  photographs remain the focus.
- Photo cards have subtle radius, restrained shadow, consistent gutters, and
  descriptive alt text.
- Clicking a photo opens an accessible lightbox with close, previous, and next.
- Include clear hover/focus states and reduced-motion compatibility.
- Do not fabricate lab events or people; use neutral placeholder blocks in the
  design draft until real photos are supplied.
